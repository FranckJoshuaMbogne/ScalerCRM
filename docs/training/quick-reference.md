# SCALER quick reference

**Address:** `https://app.<domain>`. Sign in with your work email.

## Install the app
- **Android (Chrome):** account menu → **Install the app**, or Chrome menu → *Install app*.
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Open SCALER from the home screen, then account menu → **Turn on phone notifications**.

## Where things are
| I want to… | Go to |
|------------|-------|
| See what to do today | Tasks (My day) |
| Find a customer | Search box at the top: name, phone or CIF |
| Add an enquiry | Leads → New lead |
| Reply on WhatsApp | Inbox |
| Book a visit / register a walk-in | Appointments |
| Take an order or a payment | Orders |
| See the numbers | Dashboard, Analytics |

## When something's wrong
- **"You don't have permission":** your role doesn't include it. Ask your admin.
- **"It's been more than 24 hours…":** send an approved WhatsApp template instead of a normal message.
- **"That phone number or email already belongs to another customer file":** search for it. It's the same person, so ask a manager to merge the two files.
- **No signal:** the app shows today's saved tasks. Everything else waits until you're back online. Nothing is lost.
- **Anything else:** screenshot it and send it to your admin with the time it happened.

## Signing out
On a shared phone, always **Sign out** from the account menu. It also removes the saved tasks and notifications from that phone.
