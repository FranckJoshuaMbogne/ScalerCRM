# SCALER for store and production staff

## Walk-ins
- Register walk-ins from **Appointments**. Phone number first; SCALER finds the customer if they've been before. Add what they're looking for.
- Every walk-in is also recorded on a lead. If the customer already has an open enquiry, it's added there; otherwise a new lead is opened for the staff member who served them.

## Appointments
- Book measurement, trial, alteration and delivery visits from the customer or the order.
- Mark **Completed** or **No-show** the same day. No-shows start a follow-up for the salesperson (when that automation is on).

## Measurements
- Pick the garment template, enter the measurements, save. Every version is kept with its date. Choose the right one on the order line.

## Orders and production
- Move the order through its stages as work happens: Confirmed → In production → Trial → Alterations → Ready → Delivered.
- Production staff can move stages but can't change prices or lines.
- **Ready for pickup** is the moment to message the customer (a template, if they haven't written in 24 hours).

## Payments
- Record every payment as it's received, with the method (cash, UPI, card…). Receipts are numbered automatically.
- A payment can't be more than the balance. A refund can't be more than what was paid.
- Payments are never edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected with a refund entry, so the history stays honest.
