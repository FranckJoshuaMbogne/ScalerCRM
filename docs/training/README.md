# SCALER staff training

Short guides for each kind of user, written to be read on a phone or printed. Each session takes about 45 minutes. Run it on the training business with practice data, never on live customers.

| Guide | For | Covers |
|-------|-----|--------|
| [admin.md](admin.md) | Owner / business admin | Team and roles, settings, imports, integrations, automations, audit |
| [sales.md](sales.md) | Sales executives and managers | My day, leads and pipeline, WhatsApp inbox, follow-ups, quotations |
| [store.md](store.md) | Store and production staff | Walk-ins, appointments, measurements, orders, payments |
| [quick-reference.md](quick-reference.md) | Everyone | One page: where things are and what to do when stuck |

**Before the session:**
- every trainee can sign in (invitation accepted);
- the app is installed on their phone (see quick reference);
- phone notifications are on.
