# SCALER for sales

## Start of the day: My day (Tasks)
- Overdue tasks are at the top. Clear them first.
- Each task links to its lead. Call or WhatsApp from there, then **Done** with what happened.
- Every open lead should have a next follow-up. SCALER reminds you before it's due.

## Leads
- **New lead:** phone first. If the customer already exists, SCALER attaches the lead to the same customer file (CIF).
- **Moving a stage:** drag on the board, or use the stage menu on the lead. Losing a lead asks for a reason; pick the true one, because it shapes next month's campaigns.
- **Hottest first:** sort the list by score. The score goes up with stage, value, recent activity and appointments booked.
- **Saved views:** save your usual filters once.

## WhatsApp inbox
- Reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message with normal text. After that, WhatsApp only allows approved templates.
- **Take** a conversation to make it yours. Managers can assign it.
- A customer who writes STOP is opted out automatically. Don't message them about offers.

## Quotations and orders
- **Quotation** from the lead → send → **convert to order** when accepted.
- Record the advance as a payment. The balance due shows on the order and the customer file.

## On your phone
- Install SCALER (see the quick reference) and turn on phone notifications from your account menu.
- No signal? The app shows today's tasks with call buttons, as saved the last time you were online.
